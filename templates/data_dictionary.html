Why some Field Size values are n/a
  • Numeric types do not have a fixed character length.
  • Booleans are stored as true/false with no size.
  • Date and time types represent timestamps, not fixed-length strings.
  • Foreign keys store references to other rows and do not expose a display size.
  • Binary fields and unconstrained text fields do not have a practical fixed size in the model.
  • For decimals, the size is shown as digits/scale when defined.
