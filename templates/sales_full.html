Understanding Your Sales Data

This page shows all your completed and voided transactions. Use the filters above to narrow down your search by date, user, or product type.

📋 Column Definitions
  • Sale No.: Unique identifier for each transaction. Use this when searching for specific sales.
  • Date & Time: When the transaction was recorded in the system.
  • Customer: Name of the customer who made the purchase.
  • Products: List of items sold, shown as "Product Name (Quantity/Unit)" - e.g., "Apple (125-138)" or "Mango (kilo)".
  • Items: Total number of different product types in this sale.
  • Boxes: Total quantity of boxes sold in this transaction.
💡 Understanding Columns (cont.)
  • Total: The final amount the customer paid for this transaction.
  • Recorded By: Username of the staff member who processed this sale.
  • Status: Completed = Sale was successful, Voided = Sale was cancelled by admin
🔍 Quick Tips
  • View Details: Click the three-dot menu (⋮) and select "View Details" to see full transaction information including payment breakdown.
  • Print Receipt: Generate a receipt for any completed transaction from the options menu.
  • Void Sale: Admins can cancel completed sales if there's an error or customer return (this action is logged in audit).
  • Filter by Date: Use "Today"{% if app_role == 'admin' %}, "This Week", "This Month", or set a custom date range{% else %} or "This Week"{% endif %} to analyze specific periods.

Understanding This Sale
Transaction Information
  • Sale ID: Internal database identifier for this sale.
  • Transaction No.: Unique transaction number for tracking and grouping related sales.
  • OR No.: Official Receipt number for this transaction.
  • Processed By: Staff member who recorded this sale.
Financial Breakdown
  • Subtotal: Total cost of all items before tax.
  • Plus 12% VAT: Value-Added Tax collected for BIR remittance.
  • Total Amount: Final amount customer needs to pay (Subtotal + VAT).
  • Amount Paid: Cash received from customer.
  • Change: Money returned to customer (Paid - Total).
Itemized Details
  • Description: Product name with variant (e.g., Fuji) and quantity/unit (e.g., 125-138 or kilo) in parentheses.
  • Qty: Number of boxes of this product sold.
  • Batch ID(s): Inventory batch identifiers for stock tracking and traceability.
  • Price: Price per box for this product.
  • Amount: Total for this item (Qty × Price).
