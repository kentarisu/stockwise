What does this show?

Get smart insights about your products without needing complicated calculations or extra software.

ABC Analysis - Understanding Your Top Revenue Drivers
  • Class: Category A = highest revenue contributors (usually top 70% of revenue), B = medium contributors (next 20%), C = lowest contributors (remaining 10%).
  • Product: Product name with variant and quantity/unit in parentheses.
  • Share %: Percentage of total revenue this product generates. Shows what portion of your money comes from this item.
  • Cumulative: Running total percentage when you add up all products from top to this one. Example: 94.9% means this product plus all above it make up 94.9% of your total revenue.
Slow Movers - Products Selling Very Slowly
  • Product: Items that are still selling but at a very slow pace—good candidates for discounts or promotions.
  • Quantity Sold: Total units sold during the selected period.
  • Revenue: Total money earned from this product during the period.
  • Avg / Day: Average number of units sold per day. Low numbers mean it's moving very slowly.
Dead Stock / Aging - Items Not Selling
  • Product: Items that haven't sold in a long time—consider returning them, marking them down, or using them for promotions.
  • Stock: Number of units currently sitting in your inventory unsold.
  • Last Sale: The date when this product was last sold. "No recorded sale" means it has never sold.
  • Days Idle: Number of days since the last sale. ∞ (infinity) means it has never sold. Higher numbers mean the product is collecting dust.

Understanding These Metrics:
Financial Metrics
  • Total COGS: Cost of Goods Sold - how much you paid to buy all the products you sold. Lower is better for profit.
  • Gross Profit: Money earned minus product costs (Revenue - COGS). This is your profit before expenses.
  • Net Profit: Actual money you keep after all costs. Currently same as Gross Profit (expenses tracking coming soon).
  • VAT Collected: The 12% tax you collected from customers that must be remitted to BIR.
Growth Metrics
  • Revenue Growth: How much your sales increased (or decreased) compared to the previous period. Positive % = growing, negative % = declining.
  • Txn Growth: Transaction Growth - how many more (or fewer) sales you made compared to before. Shows if you're getting more customers.
  • Sales Velocity: Average number of boxes sold per day. Higher numbers = faster inventory movement.
Operational Metrics
  • Void Rate: Percentage of transactions that were cancelled. Lower is better - high void rate may indicate pricing issues or customer dissatisfaction.
  • Peak Sales Day: The date with the highest number of sales. Use this to understand your busiest days and plan inventory accordingly.
Formula Reference
  • Gross Profit: Revenue - COGS
  • Gross Margin: (Gross Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100
  • Growth %: ((Current - Previous) ÷ Previous) × 100
  • Void Rate: (Voided ÷ Total Transactions) × 100
