Understanding Your Inventory

This page displays all your products and their current stock levels. Use the tabs to view all products, items running low on stock, or items that are out of stock.

📋 Column Definitions
  • Image: Product photo for visual identification. Click on any image to view it in full size.
  • Product ID: Unique identifier assigned to each product in your system.
  • Product: The name of the product (e.g., Apple, Mango, Orange).
  • Variant: Specific variety or type of the product (e.g., Fuji, Granny Smith, Red Delicious).
  • Quantity: Size specification or unit measurement (e.g., "125-138" for count per box, "kilo" for weight).
💡 Understanding Columns (cont.)
  • Price: Current selling price per box for this product.
  • Stock: Total number of boxes currently available in inventory.
  • Status: Available = In stock and ready to sell, Discontinued = No longer stocked
  • Options: Actions you can take - View details, Edit product info, Add stock, Change price, or Discontinue product.
📊 Inventory Tabs Explained
  • All Products: Complete list of every product in your inventory, regardless of stock level. Use this to get an overview of your entire catalog.
  • Low Stock: Products that are running low and need to be reordered soon. These items are close to their minimum stock threshold and require attention to avoid stockouts.
  • Out of Stock: Products with zero inventory. These items cannot be sold until new stock is added. Prioritize restocking these items to avoid missing sales opportunities.
🔍 Quick Tips
  • Search Products: Use the search box to quickly find products by name, variant, or ID.
  • Filter by Type: Use the "Product Type" dropdown to view only specific product categories.
  • Add New Product: Click the "+ New Product" button to register a new item in your inventory.
  • Bulk Actions: Use the "Manage Prices" and "Manage Stock" buttons to update multiple products at once.
  • Stock Alerts: Keep an eye on the "Low Stock" tab to know when to reorder products.
  • Product Details: Click the eye icon (👁️) in the Options menu to view complete product information including sales history.
⚠️ Stock Level Indicators

Stock Status Colors:

  • Green (Healthy): Stock level is sufficient. No immediate action needed.
  • Yellow/Orange (Low): Stock is running low. Consider reordering soon to avoid stockouts.
  • Red (Critical/Out): Stock is critically low or depleted. Reorder immediately to maintain sales.
